Drift is clutch-latched: hold deliberately, enter after 0.22s, release to recover. Tap and ordinary steering stay grippy.

J launches on the press edge. In the air, steer for yaw, W/S for pitch, and Space + steer for roll. Air control spends fuel; the route ramp amplifies only a jump you chose.

Z/X or the mouse wheel moves from close chase to a wide diorama view while driving. C toggles photo orbit.
